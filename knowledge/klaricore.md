## What klaricore is
amasol's IT reliability product: contract-first SLA and service level governance across many providers.

## Modules
- SLM: service level management
- XLM: experience level management
- CLM: consumption level management
- BOI: business observability intelligence

## The four pains in the klaricore pitch
1. Whose numbers are right? Each provider reports on its own definitions and tools.
2. Green SLAs, red business? Every SLA looks fine while users still suffer.
3. Who owes whom? Credits and penalties are worked out by hand, with no audit trail.
4. Which contract applies today? Versions and provider changes are hard to track.

## What the klaricore view looks for
Many service providers, a service-integrator (SIAM) role, outsourcing deals, a recent merger, regulation on third-party oversight (for example DORA for EU financial firms), SLA or vendor governance roles, SLA reporting done in spreadsheets. Phrases in job postings such as "SLA reporting in Excel", "vendor governance" or "service level manager" are klaricore signals.

## POC format
Choose two or three providers, connect contract terms and operational data, review the results.

## The three klaricore POC questions
1. Which providers and SLAs matter most?
2. Where does the data live?
3. Who would use the results?
