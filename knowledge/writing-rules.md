# Writing and content rules

- Plain business English. Short sentences. Never use em dashes.
- Lead with the customer's situation and outcomes, not with a product pitch. amasol is a trusted advisor and tool-agnostic.
- Goals are never shown as results. A customer's plan or target is a plan, not an achievement.
- Case studies and proof points come only from the product knowledge. Customer names from case studies carry a "check before naming externally" flag.
- Detectability is being faded out. Never mention it.
- Nothing may assume one country, region, regulator or industry.
- Never describe a person beyond what a cited source says.
