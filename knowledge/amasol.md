## Who amasol is
- An IT consultancy and managed service provider for observability and IT reliability.
- Founded 1999. More than 100 employees. Offices in Munich (headquarters), Vienna, London and Delhi.
- Positioning: the customer's trusted advisor. amasol is tool-agnostic. Lead with the customer's situation and outcomes, never with a product pitch.
- Product and project know-how in one team; implementation partner and managed service from one source; close personal work with application teams.

## Offerings (observability)
- Observability consulting: scalable observability platforms for large enterprises; moving operations from reactive to proactive; turning fragmented signals into a clear basis for decisions.
- Observability Trust Center: one central enterprise team (centre of excellence) that sets observability standards, governance, tooling, onboarding criteria and maturity expectations.
- Managed observability services.

## Observability maturity model (for a first guess at maturity)
- Level 1 Standardized: infrastructure monitoring and health checks.
- Level 2 Accelerated: adds alert and incident integration and performance monitoring.
- Level 3 Observability: adds log monitoring.
- Level 4 Business aligned: adds business process monitoring.

## Next-step formats
- ORS workshop (Observability Readiness Score): paid, analytical and directional. This is the format amasol pushes for.
- Assessment questionnaire: a lighter guideline option to understand requirements.

## What the amasol view looks for
Monitoring and observability tools in use, tool sprawl, SRE and operations hiring, cloud and platform migrations, outages, AI and automation initiatives.

## Typical sales path (not enforced)
Research, then first meeting and discovery, then ORS workshop or questionnaire, then recommendations, then POC.
