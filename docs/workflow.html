Workflow

Meeting preparation for amasol and klaricore sales reps
1. Create the customerCompany name
↓
2. Rep enters what they knowCompany, who is attending, their designations, agenda, good outcome, notes
↓
3. Gap checkAnything missing is asked from the rep
↓
Rep knowsAnswer is saved as final
Rep doesn't knowAdded to the research list
↓
4. Check the customer recordStart from everything already known
↓
5. ResearchWeb (websites, reports, conference pages, tenders, news), Apollo, Treg
↓
6. Make sense of itWhat matters for what we sell · confirmed vs. guess
↓
7. Meeting briefCompany · people attending · likely problems · next step
↓
Open questionsNot found by research or the rep
Curated questionsTo check guesses and move the meeting forward
↓
8. The meetingRep uses the brief and asks the questions
↓
9. Rep notes what they learnedSaved to the customer record
↺ Next meeting starts from step 4 and looks only for what is new
Rule: the rep's word is final. It overrides all research.
